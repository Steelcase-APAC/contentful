Contentful: the headless CMS for Steelcase APAC

Full APAC control of content, SEO, tagging and tracking — the business case

Adam Lau · October 2026

The problem & the fix

APAC can’t move at market speed — headless fixes that

  • Every change needs a ticket. Content edits, SEO fixes, tag updates and tracking tweaks queue behind developers and the global team — Aisah can’t even fix a meta title herself today.
  • SEO fields are inconsistent — meta titles, descriptions and canonicals vary or are missing.
  • Tagging is ad-hoc. Think of tags as labels stuck on every page and asset — “office chair”, “Singapore”, “case study”. Today each market invents its own labels, so the same content is called different things in different places. Search engines can’t make sense of it, and we can’t measure what works.
  • Headless = one content hub, every channel. Content lives in Contentful, delivered by API to the website, app and dealer portals — Aisah edits once and it flows everywhere. No code, no tickets.
The Contentful web app — a purpose-built editing interface, no code required.
What APAC gains

SEO, tagging and tracking — under our control

SEO, our way

  • Custom SEO fields on every page type — meta title, description, canonical, robots, Open Graph, slugs. Mandatory, not optional — and Aisah edits them herself, no developer needed.
  • Structured content → clean, SEO-friendly HTML; hreflang + locale URLs per market; sitemaps generated automatically.

Tagging & taxonomy

  • Content modeling: tags, categories and topics defined once as structured fields — consistent across all APAC markets. Aisah applies the shared labels as she publishes.
  • Reusable content blocks and a taxonomy that drives personalization and cleaner analytics.

Traffic tracking

  • Rebecca inserts Pixels and tracking code directly — we own the implementation layer, so tracking ships in minutes, not sprints. API + webhooks feed Adobe Analytics / GA.
  • Fast by architecture: edge-cached pages keep Core Web Vitals green; consistent taxonomy makes traffic attributable.
SEO fields become part of the editorial workflow; the content model enforces taxonomy everywhere.
APAC autonomy & the ask

APAC publishes. No tickets.

  • Granular roles: APAC editors create, review and publish — without waiting on developers or global.
  • Unlimited locales for every APAC market, managed side by side; editorial workflows with approvals — speed with guardrails.
  • Timeline: live in 2 months — content model, migration and front-end in one focused implementation.
  • Clear ownership: Aisah runs content day-to-day, Rebecca runs tracking and paid — Adam monitors the implementation, quality and the timeline.

Approve Contentful as APAC’s headless CMS — 2-month implementation of the APAC website.

From day one, APAC owns its content, SEO, tagging and tracking — and moves at market speed.
